# Flickbeam

Play local videos from your Mac on Chromecast / Google TV, or open them on an iPhone or iPad in Safari. Free, no account, no tracking.

Download the latest `.dmg` from [Releases](../../releases).

**First launch (unsigned build):** drag Flickbeam to Applications, then right-click the app, choose **Open**, and click **Open** again. Only needed once.

Apple TV and Roku support is experimental (enable in the app sidebar).

Bundles an unmodified LGPL-2.1 build of FFmpeg 7.1.1 (source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-7.1.1.tar.xz). Chromecast and Google TV are trademarks of Google LLC; Flickbeam is not affiliated with Google.
